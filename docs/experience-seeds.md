# Experience seeds v0.1

These are scoped policy seeds extracted from current `ratio` and `control-plane` experience. They are not Agent Kernel invariants and do not grant truth or authority.

## 1. Freshness before reuse

Historical provenance does not establish current qualification. Re-ground experience in the current scope, environment, subject version and evidence before relying on it.

## 2. New evidence before retry

A repeated diagnosis should consume a new discriminating reality difference. An equivalent retry without new evidence has rapidly decreasing epistemic value.

## 3. Proxy/target separation

Probe health, provider availability, scheduler state, alert resolution and other proxies prove only their own bounded proposition. They do not automatically establish the target system state.

## 4. UNKNOWN does not authorize effect

An unresolved safety/epistemic classification may justify read-class acquisition. It does not justify broadening the effect scope.

## 5. Narrow before broaden

When a failure is not localized, prefer the smallest observation or reversible action that can discriminate among live hypotheses before increasing blast radius.

## 6. Scoped generalization

A single verified episode becomes at most scoped conditional experience. General procedure/policy promotion requires repeated validation and a separate governance decision.

## 7. Representation equivalence

The line-ending synchronization incident showed that a surface-level `dirty` classification can collapse semantic change and representational noise. Before adding a new semantic failure class or blocking an effect, test a narrow deterministic equivalence relation when one is available.

## Promotion discipline

A seed may change plugin policy freely when it remains inside policy state. It should reach `agent-kernel` only when repeated real failures show that the Kernel cannot preserve a necessary semantic distinction regardless of policy quality.
